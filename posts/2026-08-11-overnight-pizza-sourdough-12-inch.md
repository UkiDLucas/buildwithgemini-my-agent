280g flour182g water42g starter5.6g saltMix flour, water, and starter until hydrated. Rest 30 minutes. Add salt and mix until incorporated. Bulk ferment 2–3 hours at room temperature with stretch-and-folds at 30, 60, 90, and 120 minutes. Shape into a round and place seam-side up in a floured banneton. Cover and refrigerate 12–24 hours. Remove from fridge and let sit 30–45 minutes. Preheat oven to 500–550°F with steel or stone for 45 minutes. Stretch dough to 12-inch disk. Top as desired. Bake 12–15 minutes until golden. Cool 2–3 minutes before slicing.  For faster room-temperature proof, skip the cold retard and proof 4–6 hours at room temperature after shaping. For longer 16–24 hour fermentation, reduce starter to 28g.
